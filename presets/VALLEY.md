# The Valley — a preset pack for erised

*The levee table from the AI-Writings game-nights canon (Nights 0–7), ported
to the mirror. Six colliding professions, one crest night, and the table's
old rule: before advice, say what your hands are doing.*

## The cast and why the tokens are what they are

The keyword vectors are the character sheets. Each one is load-bearing:

- **Three counters at one table.** Sana `count+0.6`, Odo `count+0.4`, Gant
  `count+0.5`. The Count (the canon's culture token) was never one person's
  idea — it is what happens when three count-vectors collide at a table
  under load. Watch the resonance panel: the counters should climb together
  and lift the whole field. That is the canon finding reproduced live.
- **One anti-counter.** Pell `count-0.2`. Every table needs the noun the
  token refuses; the collision between Pell and the counters is where the
  night finds its voice.
- **Opposite valences, same noun.** Sana `dark-0.2`, Ruth `dark+0.4`. Same
  word, different sign — this is the midwife/beekeeper collision the canon
  was built around. When the river takes the lights, watch who leans in.
- **The witness is the load-bearing role.** The gsim harness found that the
  Counter/Witness/Watcher always tops net shift. This cast is engineered so
  that proving it again means the fiction is honest.

## Mechanics ↔ the canon's method

| erised | the valley |
|---|---|
| scars | the failure taxonomy — broken turns stay visible; you can't patch over one |
| rewind | the re-run loop — drop the last round, keep the scar, edit the smallest token |
| resonance → ticks | the Count — culture tokens convert to time, live |
| off-screen at 0 ticks | Hollis's rule: the quiet ones are marked, not dropped |
| citations | the witness log — naming another character compounds trust |

## Running it

1. Open `index.html`, set the **levee** setting, seat the six (or seven with
   Hollis observing).
2. Tick through the crest night. Let it break where it breaks.
3. When a scar lands: rewind, change ONE keyword (the smallest token that
   answers the break), tick again. That is the method, run by hand.
4. Save the scar list. It is the night's METHOD.md entry, earned live.

*Dice are honest in the fiction; models are honest in the mirror. The
scars agree more often than you'd think — that agreement is the finding.*
